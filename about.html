About Me

Front-End Focused Full-Stack Developer & UI/UX Specialist

I am a Front-End Focused Full-Stack Developer and Technology Educator dedicated to designing, building, and refining intuitive, high-performance web applications. My work sits at the intersection of robust web architecture, clean visual design, and user-centered design principles.

My Technical Philosophy

I believe that great digital experiences are built on a foundation of clean code, responsive design systems, and seamless usability. With core full-stack competencies spanning JavaScript, React.js, Node.js, and RESTful API integration, I bridge the gap between back-end data flows and visually polished front-end interfaces. Whether translating wireframes from Figma into modular React components, optimizing layout structures with modern CSS and HTML5, or structuring data connections with Node.js and MySQL, I approach every project with strict attention to detail and web standards.

The Educator Edge

Beyond building software, I bring over a decade of higher education experience as a faculty member and program leader. Having taught courses in web development, interactive media, database fundamentals, and software logic at institutions like Century College and Globe University, I have spent years breaking down complex technical concepts into accessible, practical solutions. This background has sharpened my ability to communicate cross-functionally, collaborate effectively across teams, maintain rigorous project documentation, and continuously advocate for the end user.

Core Capabilities

  • Front-End & UI/UX: React.js, JavaScript (ES6+), HTML5, CSS3, Bootstrap, Responsive Web Design, Component Architecture, Accessibility, Figma, Adobe XD.
  • Back-End & Data: Node.js, REST/API Integration, AJAX, JSON, Firebase, MySQL, Database Design Fundamentals.
  • Interactive Media & Tools: Git/GitHub, SVG, GSAP, Animation Concepts, WordPress, Adobe Creative Cloud (Photoshop, Illustrator, After Effects).
  • Leadership & Methodology: Cross-Functional Communication, Instructional Systems Design, Design Review & Quality Assurance, Agile Problem Solving.

Education & Credentials

  • M.S. in Management: Information Technology — Globe University
